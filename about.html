Our Origins

Created to Free You from Traffic Stress

Driving through crowded metropolitan gridlocks or navigating unfamiliar highway stretches takes away valuable personal time and mental clarity. We realized that while luxury vehicle ownership is on the rise, finding a trustworthy, skilled chauffeur on demand remained frustratingly difficult.

Driver on Hire Service was established to bridge this divide — providing car owners with background-verified, polite chauffeurs trained in luxury vehicle systems, defensive driving techniques, and customer hospitality.

Our Philosophy

More Than a Driver — A Dedicated Travel Concierge

Whether safely chauffeuring your family to an outstation celebration, ensuring your children arrive comfortably at school, or welcoming an international CEO at the airport tarmac, our drivers take immense pride in their profession.

We invest heavily in ongoing chauffeur training, grooming, road safety certifications, and background verification so you can step into your car with complete peace of mind.
